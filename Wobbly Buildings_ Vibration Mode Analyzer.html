The big idea

Two words to remember

Eigenvalue = HOW FASTIt tells us the natural frequency: how many times per second the building likes to wobble.
Eigenvector = HOW IT MOVESIt tells us the shape of the wobble: which floors go left, which go right, and by how much.
K · v = ω² · M · v

K = stiffness (how hard the walls push back), M = mass (how heavy), ω² = the eigenvalue, v = the eigenvector (mode shape). The computer solves this for you every time you move a slider.
